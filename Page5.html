SURVEY AND EXAMPLES

Sustainable Food Choice Why It Is Sustainable Example in Ireland
Buy local food Reduces food miles and supports Irish farmers. Buying Irish-grown apples, potatoes, or carrots.
Choose seasonal produce Seasonal foods require less energy for growing and storage. Eating strawberries in summer or root vegetables in winter
Eat more plant-based meals Producing plant foods generally has a lower environmental impact than producing meat. Having bean chilli, lentil soup, or vegetable curry
Reduce food waste Wasting less food saves money and reduces landfill waste. Planning meals, using leftovers, and freezing extra food.
Choose foods with less packaging Reduces plastic and packaging waste. Buying loose fruit and vegetables instead of packaged ones
